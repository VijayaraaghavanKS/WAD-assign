Non-Technical Events

Event 1: Photography Contest

Description: Capture moments based on a specific theme announced at the start of the day. Show your perspective.

Rules: Individual event. Mobile phones or DSLRs are allowed. Editing tools are not permitted.

Registration Fee: Rs. 100

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 2,000 | Second Place: Rs. 1,000

Event 2: Mastermind Quiz

Description: A brain-teasing general knowledge and technical quiz tournament for quiz enthusiasts.

Rules: Teams of exactly 2. Features a preliminary written round followed by a stage-based final round.

Registration Fee: Rs. 100

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 2,000 | Second Place: Rs. 1,000

Event 3: Campus Treasure Hunt

Description: Decode cryptic clues hidden across campus to uncover the final treasure before other teams do.

Rules: Teams of 3-4 members. Safe play is mandatory; coordinates must be within college boundaries.

Registration Fee: Rs. 150

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 2,500 | Second Place: Rs. 1,500

Event 4: Esports Arena

Description: Compete in intense, fast-paced matches on popular multiplayer games in our gaming zone.

Rules: Individual/Team slots available. Knockout brackets format. Bring your own headphones.

Registration Fee: Rs. 200

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 3,000 | Second Place: Rs. 1,500
