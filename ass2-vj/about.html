About the Symposium

Objectives

Our main objective is to provide a nationwide platform for aspiring engineers to showcase their technical prowess. We aim to foster healthy competition, encourage teamwork, and bridge the gap between academic theory and practical applications.

Vision and Mission

Vision: To cultivate a culture of innovation and excellence among engineering students, making TechNova a landmark national symposium.

Mission: To nurture technical leadership and problem-solving skills through hands-on challenges, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

Organizing Department

This symposium is proudly conceptualized and managed by the student body and faculty members of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE).

Why You Should Participate

TechNova is not just a competition; it is a learning experience. You will have the opportunity to challenge your limits, benchmark your skills against peer groups from different regions, and receive feedback from industry experts.

Benefits of Participation

  • Participation certificates for all registered students.
  • Lucrative cash awards for winners and runners-up.
  • Networking opportunities with peer coders and mentors.
  • Chance to win internship referrals from our sponsors.
