Technical Competitions

Event 1: Paper Presentation

Description: Present your innovative research proposals and technical ideas before a panel of academic and industry judges.

Rules: Team size: 1-2 students. Total time: 8 minutes presentation + 2 minutes Q&A.

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 5,000 | Second Place: Rs. 3,000

Event 2: Web Design Contest

Description: Build a responsive, visually stunning website based on a surprise theme provided on-spot.

Rules: Individual event. Duration: 3 hours. Use of frameworks/libraries is restricted (HTML/CSS/JS only).

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 5,000 | Second Place: Rs. 3,000

Event 3: Coding Challenge

Description: Put your algorithms and coding skills to the test. Resolve logic puzzles and optimize programs.

Rules: Individual event. Languages allowed: C, C++, Java, or Python. Duration: 2 hours.

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 6,000 | Second Place: Rs. 4,000

Event 4: Project Expo

Description: Exhibit your working software or hardware prototypes to academic evaluators and peers.

Rules: Team size: 2-4 members. A fully working model or demonstrative prototype is mandatory.

Prize Pool: First Place: Rs. 8,000 | Second Place: Rs. 5,000
