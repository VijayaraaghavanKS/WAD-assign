Welcome to TechNova 2026

Theme: Empowering Minds, Inspiring Innovations

Date: 12th & 13th September 2026

Venue: Central Seminar Hall, Sri Krishna College of Engineering

Welcome to TechNova 2026, the prestigious national-level technical symposium organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. This year, we bring together the brightest minds from colleges across the nation to learn, compete, and share knowledge. Over the course of two action-packed days, participants will engage in technical contests, workshops, and energetic non-technical events designed to test skill and creativity.


Symposium Highlights

  • 500+ Enthusiastic Participants representing top engineering colleges.
  • 15+ Diverse Events spanning coding, designing, and gaming.
  • Exciting Cash Prizes and certificates of excellence for winners.
  • Renowned Industry Experts presiding as judges and speakers.
