About the Symposium

Objectives

To give students a platform to show their technical skills and to encourage competition between colleges.

Vision and Mission

Vision: To be a well known symposium that helps students grow.

Mission: To connect classroom knowledge to real world problems.

Organizing Department

Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

Why Students Should Participate

Students get to learn new things, meet industry people and win prizes.

Benefits of Participation

Certificates, networking, and a chance to get internship offers from sponsors.
